HOW IT WORKS

A calmer route to exam readiness.

Students always know what to do next, while parents can see meaningful progress instead of screen time alone.

01

Choose a mission

Pick a class, subject, and topic. Ignite creates a short practice path with a clear finish line.

02

Practise and review

Answer focused questions, receive explanations, and revisit the concepts that need attention.

03

Ask when stuck

Prepare a specific doubt and request help from a real teacher through a safeguarded booking flow.
