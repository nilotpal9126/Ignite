    FOR PARENTS

    Know what you are trying.

    A useful demo should make its limits as clear as its features.

    Is Ignite free? Can we try it without an account?

    This public sample demo is free and needs no registration or payment details. Paid plans, prices and cancellation terms have not been finalized; no paid subscription is offered here.

    Does this follow my child’s school syllabus?

    Today’s samples cover Physics, Chemistry and Biology for Classes 10 and 11. School-board alignment and independent review are pending. Do not treat the samples as complete syllabus coverage or exam-ready material.

    What does the result tell us?

    It shows answers marked correct against the supplied key in this small sample and lists questions to revisit. It is not an academic diagnosis or a mastery measure. A future reviewed assessment and progress history will need their own validation.

    Can we book a teacher or consultation?

    Not yet. Teacher-assisted learning is planned, but verified mentor profiles, availability, fees and safeguarding arrangements are not published. Booking and payment are unavailable in this demo. The classroom illustration in the prototype is not a real teacher profile.

    Do we need to share our child’s details?

    No. The public demo needs no name or contact information. Its answers are kept only in this page’s memory and clear on reload. New student admissions are paused while account and privacy checks are completed; do not enter real children’s details into prototype forms.

    Is short practice a replacement for school or tuition?

    This is a supplementary practice experience, not a replacement for teaching or a guarantee of better marks. Try it alongside the learning support your child already receives and judge whether the explanations are useful.
